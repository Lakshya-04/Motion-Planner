- `manipulation_manager`: `ToolFeedExecutionPort` and the `tool_actuation_feed` parameter: with a non-contact tool the spray stroke's trajectory becomes the tool executor's window, fed with the trajectory time (elapsed wall time) and cancelled on failure; the manager then leaves valve opening to `tool_actuation_node`. - MuJoCo sim: `UNEXPECTED_CONTACT` monitor (self collision and arm-link-on-world contacts, tool on the wall excluded), logged per new body pair and summarised at shutdown (`docs/sim_mujoco.md`). The pass score does not check these.

### Fixed

- `reachability` review fixes. `Map4D`: a vertical tool axis is keyed at y* = 0 exactly and `inverseQuery` reads that one row (it read a row below the recorded one on the default map, nothing on 0.1 m cells, and emitted rings twice when noise split a pose over two rows). `selectStands`: the 2-opt time budget starts with the tour step, not at the start of scoring (the result no longer depends on machine speed through scoring time); `pick_index` is the position in the cover's pick order (it was the index in the compacted list); a non-finite `margin_penalty` is refused. Horizontal stands: the search reaches max(`margin`, `xy_extent`) beyond the targets (a lone target reachable only beyond 0.7 m was reported unreachable), `heightWithinLiftReach` uses the map's rounded-up window, stands take the base's arrival yaw instead of a forced 0 (no phantom final turn in `travel_s`), and a bad `lift_step` / `lift_max` / `grid_step` gives `HorizontalStandCode::kInvalidRequest`. `stationGridProblem`, `heightRange` and `makeCandidateStands` refuse non-finite steps and grids over 250000 points instead of looping or running out of memory. `rm4d_tool` rejects a zero or non-finite step and a trailing option without a value. Tests: 12 new gtests across map4d, lift_bands, station, global_stands and horizontal_stands, plus a brute-force check of the stand-count bound and of 2-opt.

- cuRobo backend review, findings verified one by one (PR #113): the node no longer dies on an exception in any service handler (failed response, code `internal_error`); `ik_weights` zeros are kept and the length is checked at construction; `voxelize_points` raises instead of returning an oversized cube when the cap is 0 or cannot be met; `CuroboClientPlanner::collision` no longer checks the line from `joint_values` to the first trajectory state; `PlanJoint` / `PlanPose` carry `route` and a graph-seed plan reaches the C++ result as `status == "success_graph_seed"`; a failed world update makes the client refuse `plan` / `ik` / `collision` (`world_stale`) instead of planning on the old world; the cuRobo and VAMP plugins read a `base_frame` param; client `timeout_sec` default 30 -> 45 s; `check_motion` step-count cap, unknown `prismatic_joints` rejected, and helper-script fixes (see the PR).
- cuRobo `plan_cspace` failed every UR10e benchmark problem with a dock pose as an endpoint (52 to 62 of 158): trajectory optimisation pushed the `arm_forearm_link` / `arm_wrist_2_link` spheres 0.5 to 1.7 mm into each other (a pair two joints apart that the generated collision matrix did not ignore) and the dense validation rejected every attempt. `robot_assets.py` now passes `--ignore-hops` (its `--hops`, default 2, as for VAMP and OMPL) to `generate_curobo_robot.py` (158/158 on a regenerated config; regenerate older cuRobo configs). `CuroboPlanner` and the node got `seed_fallback` (default on), `fallback_attempts` and `max_attempts`: when trajectory optimisation returns nothing the first validated graph seed is returned with `PlanResult.route == 'graph_seed'`. Evidence and numbers: `docs/planner_benchmark.md`. Unit tests with a stubbed planner plus a GPU test on Franka; not re-run: the node, C++ client and sim.
- `CuroboPlanner.check_states` raised `AttributeError` on a planner built with `graph_planner_algorithm='rrt'` (the GPU RRT planner keeps the feasibility check on its `resources`); it now falls back to that.
- Multi-stand pass, second stand (PR #110): the stand-1 approach was refused about every other run (`collision truncation` arm_upper_arm_link <-> lift_carriage_link, then `recovery jog refused`, or the tool resting on the wall's top edge). `manipulation_manager` seeds the dock IK (`dock_seed_joint_state`, `dock_ik_seeded`), checks the tool reached the approach pose before docking and re-primes or jogs back otherwise (`verify_approach`, `approach_tolerance_m`), and logs the arm state at stand arrival and before each dock; the MuJoCo multi-stand passes load `walls_sim.pcd` (the whole sim wall plus an occupied plane behind it) into MoveIt instead of the contact patch. Same-session rerun, 8 runs per scenario and code state: `two_panels_plain` 6 of 8 pass on `origin/main`, 8 of 8 on the branch; `two_panels` 8 of 8 both; collision truncations 5 to 66 per run on `origin/main`, 0 to 6 on the branch. Not shown: that the difference in pass rate is significant, or that the Gazebo pass is fixed (it probably shares the cause).
- Wall rasterizer blocked one cell more than the inflated obstacle overlaps (the cell past `ceil(max)`), so the planner's keep-out was up to 0.02 m wider per side than the scorer's 0.07 m clearance and a strip beside a small obstacle was never wiped; the clearance itself is unchanged. MuJoCo scenario reruns (12 runs, `docs/sim_mujoco.md`): single-stand scenarios 8 of 8 pass (protrusion cov reachable 0.884 -> 0.983, window 0.793 -> 1.000), multi-stand 2 of 4 pass (stand 1 approach fails in about half of the runs, cause open).
- `CoverageOutputJsonSource`: a one-cell gap stroke becomes a short stroke instead of being dropped. `run_manager_pass.sh` takes `MANAGER_PASS_PLANNER_PARAMS`, `run_scenarios.sh` `SCEN_DECOMP`; `run_multi_stand_pass.sh` takes `MULTI_STAND_FILM=1`.
- Gazebo multi-stand pass: `scripts/wait_arm_settled.py` blocks until the arm joints stop creeping (the gz position loop under `jtac_position_only` has a 10 s time constant) and runs in front of `stand_arrival_command` in `run_multi_stand_pass.sh` (`MULTI_STAND_SETTLE_TIMEOUT`). 3 measured passes on a loaded host: 6/10, 10/10, 10/10 columns, coverage 0.60 / 0.94 / 1.00, peaks 36 / 54 / 3025 N (before: 1 of 2 to 1 of 3 passing); the remaining failure (dock plans refused after an in-stand approach) is not fixed, see `docs/sim_gazebo.md`.
- VAMP robot model rejected most benchmark endpoints (60 of 72 in the empty scene, every `stow_side` pair): FOAM's 6 spheres on the 0.04 m lift carriage plate bulged into the upper arm. `docker/tools/generate_vamp_robot.sh` now defaults `FOAM_ARGS` to `--lift_carriage_link=4` (23 plate spheres, 50 in total); on the same 158 problems VAMP goes from 10/158 to 158/158. Numbers in `docs/planner_benchmark.md`.
